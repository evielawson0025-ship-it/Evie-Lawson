Comprehensive Book Marketing Solutions

Deep Market Research & Analysis

Personalized Author Outreach

Custom-crafted cold outreach campaigns using data-backed messaging that resonates with your target audience. Every pitch is tailored to demonstrate genuine understanding of each author's unique challenges and opportunities—no generic templates ever.

Strategic Email Marketing

Full-funnel email sequences designed to nurture readers from first discovery through loyal fandom. Automated journeys that transform single-book buyers into complete-series collectors, dramatically increasing customer lifetime value.

Social Media & Influencer Campaigns

Strategic BookTok seeding, Bookstagram partnerships, and targeted influencer outreach to amplify your launch. I identify and coordinate with micro-influencers whose audiences align perfectly with your genre, creating authentic buzz that algorithms reward.

Amazon & Retail Optimization

Complete marketplace domination strategy including metadata optimization, A+ content development, strategic keyword targeting, and timed promotional campaigns. We position your book for maximum visibility in search results and recommendation algorithms.

Series Launch Architecture

Strategic planning for multi-book releases that build momentum with each installment. Cross-series audience migration strategies, intelligent bundling recommendations, pre-order funnels, and launch timing optimized for your specific genre's buying patterns.

Review Generation Systems

Structured reviewer outreach programs and automated review request sequences that help you reach critical review milestones faster. Strategic timing of review campaigns to maximize algorithmic impact during key sales windows.

Press & Media Relations

Performance Analytics & Reporting

Weekly comprehensive KPI dashboards tracking every metric that matters to your success. Clear, actionable recommendations based on real-time data so you're always informed and can make confident decisions about your marketing investment.
